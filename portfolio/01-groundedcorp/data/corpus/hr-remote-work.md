Employees in hybrid-eligible roles are expected on site a minimum of 3 days per calendar week.
Fully remote status requires VP approval and is reviewed every 6 months against role requirements.
Remote workers must maintain a broadband connection of at least 25 Mbps downstream and work from the country recorded in the HR system of record.
Working from a country other than the recorded one for more than 30 consecutive days triggers a payroll and tax review and must be approved in advance.
Home office equipment is reimbursed up to 30,000 INR once every 3 years against submitted invoices.
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 in the employee's registered time zone; meetings outside core hours must be optional.
Managers may revoke hybrid eligibility for performance reasons only after a documented conversation and a 30 day improvement window.
