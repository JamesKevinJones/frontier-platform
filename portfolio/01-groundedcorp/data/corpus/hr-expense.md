dept: hr
doc_type: policy
version: 2025.1

Expense claims must be submitted within 45 days of the transaction date; claims older than 90 days are rejected without exception.
Domestic air travel is booked in economy class; business class requires director approval and a flight duration exceeding 6 hours.
Daily meal allowance is 1,500 INR for metro cities and 1,000 INR for non-metro locations, and receipts are required above 500 INR.
Client entertainment requires pre-approval and the names of all attendees recorded on the claim.
Personal expenses combined on a shared receipt must be itemised and deducted before submission.
Reimbursement is processed in the next payroll cycle after manager approval, typically within 15 business days.
Repeated policy violations are escalated to the employee's manager and may result in withdrawal of corporate card privileges.
