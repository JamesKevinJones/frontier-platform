A security incident is any unauthorized access, malware infection, data exposure, or denial of service affecting company systems or data.
Severity SEV-1 incidents (active breach or customer data exposure) must be escalated to the Incident Commander within 15 minutes.
Containment steps include isolating affected hosts, revoking compromised credentials, and preserving forensic evidence.
Communications to customers about confirmed personal data breaches follow Legal guidance and applicable regulations.
Incident postmortems are mandatory for SEV-1 and SEV-2 and must be published within 5 business days.
Phishing reports should be forwarded to security@example.com; do not click links or open attachments in suspicious mail.
