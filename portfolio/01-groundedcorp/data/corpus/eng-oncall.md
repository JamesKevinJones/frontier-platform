On-call rotations run for one week starting Wednesday at 10:00 and no engineer may be scheduled for more than one week in any four week period.
The primary on-call engineer must acknowledge a page within 5 minutes; unacknowledged pages escalate to the secondary after 10 minutes.
If the secondary does not acknowledge within a further 10 minutes, the page escalates to the engineering manager.
On-call engineers must have a working laptop, a charged phone and network access for the duration of the rotation.
Any page that results in work outside business hours is compensated according to the on-call allowance schedule.
A handover note summarising open issues, ongoing incidents and known risks is mandatory at the end of every rotation.
Engineers may swap on-call shifts with a peer provided the change is recorded in the scheduling tool at least 24 hours in advance.
