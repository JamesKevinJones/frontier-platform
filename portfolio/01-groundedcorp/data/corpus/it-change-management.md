Every production change requires an approved change record before deployment; emergency changes may be recorded within 24 hours after the fact.
Standard changes follow a weekly Change Advisory Board that meets on Wednesday and requires submission 48 hours before the meeting.
A change freeze applies from December 20 to January 2 and during declared major incidents; only emergency changes are permitted.
Every change record must include a rollback plan, a validation step and a named accountable owner.
Changes affecting customer-facing systems require a documented blast radius assessment and a staged rollout starting at 5 percent of traffic.
Failed changes must be rolled back within 30 minutes if service level objectives are breached.
Post-implementation review is mandatory for any change that caused an incident, and is due within 5 business days.
