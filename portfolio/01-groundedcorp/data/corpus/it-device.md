title: Endpoint and Device Management Standard

All corporate laptops must be enrolled in mobile device management before first network access and must report compliance every 24 hours.
Full disk encryption is mandatory on every endpoint; a device reporting as unencrypted is quarantined from corporate resources automatically.
Operating system patches rated critical must be installed within 7 days of release; all other patches within 30 days.
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk within 4 hours of discovery so that a remote wipe can be issued.
A remote wipe of a personally owned device enrolled in the bring-your-own-device programme requires written employee consent captured at enrolment.
Local administrator rights are granted only through a time-bound elevation request that expires after 8 hours.
Devices that have not checked in for 45 days are marked dormant and their certificates are revoked.
